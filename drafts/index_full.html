How it works

  1. 1
    Tell us the deal. From your phone, pick the stage you are at. Only the fields that matter show up, in the form's own words, with the full document a tap away.
  2. 2
    We build the packet. Your answers fill the OREC form set for that stage, on the edition in force the day you send it, including the disclosures that have to travel with it.
  3. 3
    You sign first. The packet comes to you before anyone else. Read it, confirm it, pass it on. Nothing reaches a client that you have not seen.
  4. 4
    Your clients sign. Electronic signature, routed in order, with reminders that come from us rather than from you.
  5. 5
    Filed the same day. Executed documents go into your brokerage's document storage, which stays the record of the transaction.
  6. 6
    You get a daily digest. Where every contract sits, what is due next, inspection and deadline reminders, and what the other side is waiting on.

Questions agents ask

Which forms do you work with?

The Oklahoma Real Estate Commission's published form set: residential sale and lease, the disclosures that travel with them, and the mid-transaction forms. Reference documents such as the RPCD Act and the lead-paint pamphlet are delivered to clients where required and are never part of a signing packet.

Do I lose control of my transaction?

No. Every packet comes to you first. You read it, confirm it, and pass it on. If something is wrong it stops with you, before a client ever sees it.

Where do the documents live afterward?

With your brokerage. Executed copies are uploaded to your brokerage's document storage the same day they are complete. That copy is the legal record. We keep working files only through execution and a short re-send buffer.

Who is behind this?

Two licensed Oklahoma real-estate professionals who built the service for their own transactions first.
