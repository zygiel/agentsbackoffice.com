How it works

  1. Tell us the deal from your phone, at the stage you are at.
  2. We build the packet on the OREC edition in force the day you send it, with the disclosures that travel with it.
  3. You sign first. Nothing reaches a client you have not seen.
  4. Your clients sign in order, with reminders that come from us rather than from you.
  5. Filed within one business day with your brokerage, which stays the record of the transaction.
  6. You see the status in the portal as the deal moves.

We are onboarding agents by invitation. If you were referred here by one of us, use the number you already have and we will set up your account.
